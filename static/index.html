📱 How to use Journa NextGen on iPhone / iOS

  1. Make sure your iPhone and Windows PC are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Find your PC's IP address (e.g. 192.168.1.XX).
  3. Open Safari on iPhone and navigate to: http://<PC-IP>:8000
  4. Tap the Share button () at the bottom of Safari.
  5. Select "Add to Home Screen".
  6. Now Journa NextGen opens as a native app on your iPhone! All trades synced from MT5 on PC are visible on your iPhone in real-time.
